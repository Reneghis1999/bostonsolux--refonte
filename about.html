Focus on solutions.

BostonSolux is a business development consultancy committed to excellence in client service. We implement fully integrated enterprise solutions as well as standalone products, with proven business processes to address our clients' challenges in every area of their business.

What our clients can count on

Three strengths, and one rule we apply to every project: speed and efficiency.

  • Depth of consulting

    We start from your challenges, not from a catalogue, and recommend the solutions and processes that fit your organisation.

  • Diversity of skills

    Networks, security, ERP, payments, project management and training: one partner for the whole of your information system.

  • Adaptability

    Integrated enterprise solutions or standalone products, on-site or remote: we adapt to meet each client's expectations.

Growing with Africa

BostonSolux is expanding its business to serve the African market, where companies' needs for services and technology are growing considerably.

From our headquarters in Rockville and our office in Paris, we support clients through offices in Lomé, N'Djamena, Malabo, Conakry and Cotonou. In Equatorial Guinea, BostonSolux is the number one business and IT partner.

Milestones

  1. 2009

    Opening in Lomé

    BostonSolux launches its business at its Lomé office, in Togo.

  2. 2013

    A new home in Lomé

    With more staff and services, the Lomé teams move into a more spacious building.

  3. 2015

    MMIE goes live

    The Ministry of Mines, Industry and Energy in Malabo goes live with Bsecured Park, on time and on budget.

  4. 2022

    Partnership with PECB

    PECB and BostonSolux sign a partnership agreement.

  5. 2023

    ITIL ambassador

    With PeopleCert, BostonSolux recognises consultant Emmanuel Amou as an ambassador of the ITIL framework.
