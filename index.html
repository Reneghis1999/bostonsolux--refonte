Case study

The Ministry of Mines, Industry and Energy goes live with Bsecured Park

Client
Ministry of Mines, Industry and Energy (MMIE), Malabo, Equatorial Guinea
Challenge
Move to new offices in a highly secured environment, with modern tools from day one.
Solution
Bsecured Park, on Cisco and Microsoft: secured telecommunications, dedicated email, advanced telephony and video, Linux servers and storage in a robust data centre, and Voice over IP ready to connect other government sites.
Outcome
Delivered on time and on budget.
