Twelve services, one team from design to support.

We analyse your needs, design the right architecture, implement it and keep it running. Our services are organised in four domains, so you can find the right expertise quickly.

  • Infrastructure & Networks
  • Security & Continuity
  • Business Applications
  • Consulting & Transformation

Domain 1 of 4

Infrastructure & Networks

The foundations your teams work on every day: networks, directories, workstations and equipment.

  • Network Design & Implementations

    We analyse your current network and design an effective, economical solution based on best practices, ensuring the confidentiality, availability and integrity of your data.

  • Active Directory Design & Implementations

    Custom design and implementation of Microsoft Active Directory services, to lower the total cost of ownership of your directory and improve its return on investment.

  • Corporate Desktop Deployments and Support

    Centralised, automated deployments and full lifecycle support for PCs, laptops and handheld devices, on-site, off-site or in a hybrid model.

  • Hardware & Software Application Solutions

    Integrated or stand-alone hardware and software platforms, chosen and implemented to improve productivity and reduce operating costs.

Domain 2 of 4

Security & Continuity

Protect your information system and keep it running, whatever happens.

  • Network Security

    Our security engineers identify your needs, build rigorous methods to protect your operations and strengthen your overall information security.

  • Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery

    A free risk assessment, then a continuity of operations plan (COOP) tailored to your business and your budget.

  • Enterprise Backup Solutions

    Backup, disaster recovery and data protection on a unified platform for physical and virtual environments, with partners such as Acronis.

Domain 3 of 4

Business Applications

The applications that run your operations, your communications and your payments.

  • ERP & PLM Implementations

    We find and implement the ERP and PLM solutions that fit your business processes, with certified consultants and checklists that catch risks early.

  • Enterprise Messaging Implementations

    Reliable enterprise messaging that keeps you connected 24/7, even during scheduled downtime, while lowering total cost of ownership.

  • Monetics

    Online and mobile payments, payment terminals, ATMs and the integration of electronic payment systems, built to the highest security standards.

Domain 4 of 4

Consulting & Transformation

Plan and steer change, from digital strategy to project delivery.

  • Digital Transformation

    Cloud solutions, data and artificial intelligence, digital consulting and modern applications, to help your organisation adapt and innovate.

  • Project Management

    Our PMO sets project management standards, makes sure they are applied and keeps management informed, so projects finish on time and on budget. Looking for a PMP? Write to pmo@bostonsolux.com.
