Bsecured Park

A blended solution on the latest Cisco and Microsoft technologies to secure your telecommunications infrastructure, improve your internet browsing and modernise your communications.

Talk to our team See it in production

What Bsecured Park delivers

One integrated solution for security, communications and hosting.

  • A secured infrastructure

    Your telecommunications infrastructure is protected with the latest Cisco and Microsoft technologies, for reliable access to your organisation's resources.

  • Modern communications

    Dedicated email, advanced telephony and video give your teams the tools to communicate and work efficiently.

  • A data centre ready to grow

    Linux servers and storage systems become part of a robust data centre, able to host other applications such as document management and Quick Billing.

Ready to connect all your sites

Voice over IP is a key component of Bsecured Park. It prepares your organisation to connect all its business locations in a single system.

Once the implementation is complete, your offices are equipped with the tools they need to work efficiently, on an infrastructure built to support your next projects.

Included in the solution

  • Secured telecommunications infrastructure
  • Improved internet browsing
  • Dedicated email
  • Advanced telephony and video
  • Linux servers and storage systems
  • Voice over IP across sites
