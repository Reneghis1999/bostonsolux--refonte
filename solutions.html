  • Bsecured Park

    For organisations and public institutions

    A blended solution on the latest Cisco and Microsoft technologies that secures your telecommunications infrastructure and modernises your communications.

    • Dedicated email, advanced telephony and video
    • Linux servers and storage in a robust data centre
    • Voice over IP to connect all your sites
    • Ready for document management and Quick Billing
    Discover Bsecured Park

  • BSmartGrid

    For utility companies

    An integrated utility business management solution that gives executives a clear view of system performance, from outages to billing.

    • Executive dashboard of utility performance
    • Outage supervision and asset management
    • Middleware based on SCADA standards
    • Billing, capacity planning and call centre integration
    Discover BSmartGrid

  • Mobile Banking

    For banks and financial institutions

    Financial services on the mobile phone, reaching customers outside traditional banking channels, with a full suite of electronic payments.

    • Send and receive money, cash-in and cash-out
    • Bill payments, shopping and airtime top-up
    • KYC, anti-money laundering and CFT settings
    • Mastercard, MoneyGram and Western Union integration
    Discover Mobile Banking

  • Bsales Pro

    For bars, restaurants, dry cleaners and hotels

    A point of sale that runs your day-to-day business, now with integrated payroll.

    • Hotel booking and room management
    • Stock management and invoicing
    • Customer relationship management
    • Payroll
    Discover Bsales Pro

  • SSLoGe

    For banks

    A dedicated secure link between a bank's ATMs and its headquarters or branches, adding SSL encryption over 3G/4G connectivity.

    • SSL encryption of the 3G/4G link
    • Data integrity and reliability
    • Designed for ATM connectivity
    • Strong cryptography, public and private keys
    Discover SSLoGe

  • Quick Billing

    For telecom operators

    A modular billing application built around a standard billing module, with prepaid and postpaid managed on one platform.

    • Mediation for Huawei, Ericsson, Alcatel and ZTE
    • Rating, billing and roaming (TAP, NRTRDE)
    • Value-added services and interconnection billing
    • Convergent prepaid and postpaid billing
    Discover Quick Billing
